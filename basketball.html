Head Coach · Almas Ismail

Development is personal

At Arceni Basketball, we believe sports, basketball in particular – is an entire universe in itself, where hearts beat, passion breathes, growth and untapped human potential lies. Basketball is a safe space, where you can engage, learn about yourself, your capabilities – a place where anything you set your mind to can be manifested. It's not always about wins, but the pursuit for growth, greatness – as an individual, a team, community and society.

My philosophy is enshrined in building a strong foundation for aspiring basketball players, fostering communities that embody character, discipline, teamwork and grit. I believe that players grow best when development starts with understanding who they are, how they play, and where they want to go.

On court, the work is practical and focused: learn the skill, apply it under pressure, and carry that growth into competition and everyday life.
