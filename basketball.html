Frequently Asked Questions

FAQs

Whether you’re a parent, a player, or looking for support for your organization, start with the questions that matter to you.

Is your program a good fit for my child?

Share your child’s age, experience and goals with us. We can talk through their needs and the available player-development options before you decide whether to enroll. Send an inquiry to start the conversation.

Do I need to be an experienced player?

Not necessarily. Training is player-centered, and the starting point depends on each player’s current skills and goals. Get in touch to discuss your child’s experience and what support may be appropriate.

How can you help me improve my game?

We focus on fundamentals, game awareness, confidence and decision-making. We’ll discuss your goals and identify areas to work on through focused, personalized coaching.

Can you support a team or organization?

Yes. Our consulting services can be tailored to a team’s or organization’s development and performance needs. Tell us about your program, who it serves and what you hope to strengthen.

Do you do speaking engagements on development?

Yes. Send details about your event, intended audience, date and the development topics you have in mind, and we can discuss the opportunity.

How can I register to engage with you?

Use the basketball inquiry form and select the role that best describes you. Include your goals, timeline and any relevant context so we can follow up with next steps.
