Alexander Arceni

Founder · Managing Partner

Alexander Arceni founded ARCENI after two decades at the intersection of elite performance, institutional strategy, and leadership development. His career has spanned senior advisory roles with Fortune 500 boards, professional sports franchises, and family offices across three continents.

What began as a personal practice — working privately with leaders navigating their most consequential decisions — evolved into a firm built on a single conviction: the discipline that produces enduring athletes produces enduring executives. The same rigor, the same standards, the same refusal to settle.
