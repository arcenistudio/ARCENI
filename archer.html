Frequently Asked Questions

FAQs.

Whether you’re supporting a young person, finding your own direction, or ready to bring a project to life. Talk with us.

How can the Archer program support my child?

The Archer gives young people space to reflect, identify their strengths and interests, and shape goals in their own words. A parent or supporter can help start the conversation, while the young person remains at the center of the coaching process.

What would coaching with you look like?

Coaching offers a supportive space to slow down, explore what matters, and break a big question into manageable next steps. It isn’t a promise of a quick fix or a substitute for clinical or mental-health care when that is what someone needs.

Do I need to know what I want to do with my life?

No. You can begin with uncertainty. The Archer helps you take stock of your strengths, values and experience, then explore options and define a next step that feels realistic and meaningful.

Is the program only about choosing a career?

No. The process can also help you clarify what you want to contribute, build confidence in your ideas, and map practical first steps for a project or initiative.

How do I know if I’m ready for the program?

You don’t need a finished plan. It helps to be open to reflection, conversation and trying steps between sessions. If you’re unsure, get in touch and we can talk through what support might fit.

How long is the program and how can I enroll?

The Archer typically runs for 3–6 months and can be delivered in person or virtually. The next intake is planned for December 2026. Send an inquiry to ask about availability and next steps.
