The Archer Program · FAQ

Is The Archer right for you?

The program works best when the coaching approach and timing match what you need right now.

This is perfect for you if...

  • You’re a young person or aspiring professional navigating a transition or exploring what comes next.
  • You want to better understand your strengths, values and interests.
  • You’re open to reflection, new perspectives and setting goals that feel meaningful to you.
  • You can make space to take part in coaching and try purposeful next steps over time.

This is not for you if...

  • You’re looking for a guaranteed job placement or someone to make career decisions for you.
  • You need an immediate solution rather than a process of reflection and action.
  • You’re not able to commit time to coaching and follow-through at this point.
  • You need support beyond what a coaching program can provide right now.
