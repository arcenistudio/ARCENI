The Coach · ARCENI Basketball

Meet Almas.

Almas Ismail is the founder of ARCENI, a coach and consultant committed to helping people and organizations move with purpose. Through basketball, he develops strong fundamentals, confidence and character that extend beyond the court.

His approach combines focused practice, honest feedback and a belief that every player can grow through discipline, teamwork and consistent action.

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQs

Whether you’re a player, parent, coach or organization, start with the questions that matter to you.

Is player development a good fit for me?

Share your current experience, goals and what you want to improve. We can talk through the right starting point before you commit.

Do I need to be an experienced player?

Not necessarily. Training is player-centered, and the starting point depends on your current skills, goals and development needs.

What does a development session focus on?

Sessions can focus on fundamentals, game awareness, confidence, decision-making and the habits that support growth on and off the court.

Can you work with a team or organization?

Yes. Consulting support can be tailored to a team’s or organization’s development and performance needs. Learn more about consulting.

How do I get started?

Use the Basketball page to learn more about the programs and send an inquiry with your goals and timeline.

Do you offer speaking or workshops?

Yes. Share your event, intended audience, date and development topics, and we can discuss the right format.
